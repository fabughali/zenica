# 🏥 Zenica Healthcare - Comprehensive Digital Health Platform

**Transform your healthcare experience with cutting-edge technology and seamless multi-role integration.**

Zenica Healthcare is a revolutionary Flutter-based digital health platform that brings together patients, doctors, pharmacists, delivery agents, and insurance providers in one unified ecosystem. Experience the future of healthcare with AI-powered symptom checking, real-time video consultations, digital prescription management, and intelligent delivery tracking.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Symptom Checker
- **Intelligent Diagnosis**: Advanced AI algorithms analyze symptoms with interactive body diagrams
- **Voice Input Support**: Speak your symptoms naturally with voice recognition technology
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual progress indicators guide you through the assessment process
- **Comprehensive Results**: Detailed analysis with potential conditions and recommendations

### 🎥 Video Consultation Platform
- **HD Video Calls**: Crystal-clear video consultations with doctors worldwide
- **Real-time Chat**: Instant messaging during consultations for better communication
- **Call Quality Indicators**: Monitor connection quality for optimal experience
- **Prescription Creation**: Doctors can create digital prescriptions during consultations
- **Consultation Tools**: Screen sharing, drawing tools, and medical calculators

### 💊 Digital Prescription Management
- **Smart Scanning**: QR code and barcode scanning for prescription verification
- **Digital Storage**: Secure cloud-based prescription storage and management
- **Refill Reminders**: Automated notifications for prescription renewals
- **Medication Tracking**: Monitor medication adherence and side effects
- **Pharmacy Integration**: Direct connection to local pharmacies for fulfillment

### 🚚 Intelligent Delivery Tracking
- **Real-time GPS Tracking**: Live location updates for medication deliveries
- **Interactive Maps**: Google Maps integration with delivery routes
- **Proof of Delivery**: Digital signatures and photo confirmation
- **Delivery Controls**: Schedule, reschedule, and manage delivery preferences
- **Status Notifications**: Real-time updates on delivery progress

### 🏥 Multi-Role Dashboard System
- **Patient Dashboard**: Health summaries, appointment scheduling, emergency contacts
- **Doctor Dashboard**: Patient management, schedule coordination, medical statistics
- **Pharmacy Dashboard**: Order processing, inventory management, prescription fulfillment
- **Insurance Dashboard**: Claims processing, policy management, coverage verification
- **Delivery Dashboard**: Route optimization, delivery management, customer communication

### 🛡️ Insurance Claims Processing
- **Automated Processing**: AI-powered claim validation and processing
- **Document Scanning**: OCR technology for medical document processing
- **Bulk Operations**: Process multiple claims simultaneously
- **Real-time Status**: Track claim status from submission to approval
- **Digital Verification**: Secure document verification and fraud detection
